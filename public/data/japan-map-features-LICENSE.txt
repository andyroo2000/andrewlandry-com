japan-transfers.json
- Sapporo station (OSM node 4086852685) to Fukagawa station (node 264420469):
  shortest connected railway alignment through the Hakodate Main Line corridor.
  Station endpoints are projected onto the railway, with 8-metre simplification.
  The file lists all 120 constituent OSM ways.
- Wakkanai–Oshidomari ferry: OSM way 122776536.
- Oshidomari–Kafuka ferry: OSM way 122776521, also used in reverse.
- Sapporo–Asahikawa railway: connected rail alignment via Fukagawa from OSM
  ways listed in the file, simplified to 5 metres. Used in both directions.
  Both train journeys are stated in https://bike.andrewlandry.com/2026/.
- New Chitose–Sapporo airport bus: an illustrative standard downtown corridor
  assembled from OSM driving roads, respecting mapped one-way directions, via
  the Do-o Expressway, Kitahiroshima exit, and Route 36 to Susukino. This is not
  the user's recorded bus track or a verified specific service/boarding stop.
  Corridor reference: https://www.hokto.co.jp/sapporo-chitose/.
- Ground connections: OSM street paths for Sapporo city/station, Fukagawa and
  Asahikawa station access, Wakkanai port access, and gaps between the
  Teshikaga and Ashoro ride recordings. Short straight links connect adjacent
  recording endpoints or station/port entrances where no track was recorded.
  These links are illustrative and excluded from cycling totals.
- The bus and street geometries list their constituent OSM ways, simplified
  to 5 metres. Original responses: bus-roads, local-roads, rail-asahikawa.
- Coordinates use longitude/latitude and six decimal places.
- The site's illustrative Haneda–New Chitose flight arc is calculated separately
  between the airport media coordinates. It is not an OSM aviation route or a
  recorded flight track.

hokkaido-lakes.json
- 26 named lakes, lagoons, reservoirs, and ponds, selected within Hokkaido.
- Original OSM way/relation IDs are retained in each feature's properties.
- Multipolygon outer rings and island holes are assembled from member ways.
- Boundaries are simplified to 15 metres, retaining polygon topology.
- Six-decimal longitude/latitude. Exterior rings are clockwise for D3's
  spherical polygon convention; interior rings have the opposite winding.

Private processing scripts and original Overpass responses are retained locally
under Movies/Japan Bike Trips/work/map-data/transfers. All derived geographic
data used by the page are downloadable from this directory under ODbL.
