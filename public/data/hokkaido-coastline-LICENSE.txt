Transformations: union the simplified EPSG:3857 land polygons; clip to
138–148 degrees east, 34–48 degrees north (including arrival at Haneda); convert to longitude/latitude;
round to six decimals. Exterior rings use D3's clockwise spherical winding
(opposite to RFC 7946). Retain interior rings and island polygons.

The resulting database is hokkaido-coastline.json in this directory, made
available under ODbL 1.0. This license applies to this coastline database,
not Andrew Landry's separate Strava route recordings or video media.
